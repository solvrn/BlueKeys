# BlueKeys ⌨️

Turn your Android phone into a Bluetooth keyboard. Paste text on your phone, and it types it into any computer.

## [⬇️ Download BlueKeys](https://github.com/solvrn/BlueKeys/releases/latest/download/BlueKeys.apk)

Tap the link on your phone, then tap the file and **Install**. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser. (Android 14 or newer.)

## Use
1. Open BlueKeys, tap **Connect**, and pair with your computer's Bluetooth.
2. Paste your text, click where you want it on the computer, tap **Type it**.

**Turbo** types as fast as possible. **Natural** types at a human pace. **Code mode** keeps code editors from adding extra brackets and indents.

Your text only goes to the computer you paired, over Bluetooth.
